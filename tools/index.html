Tools

Everyday engineering tools. Each one runs entirely in your browser: the files you choose are read on your device and the results are built there. Nothing is uploaded.

Parts List Extractor Turn a pile of spare parts workbooks into one long table. Finds the header row wherever it sits, expands merged cells, unpivots the model columns. Coil Data Extractor Turn coil selection quotations in Word or PDF into one Excel table. One row per coil, every physical, air, fluid and pricing field as a column with its unit. Container Calculator Work out how many containers or trailers a shipment needs, with a load plan, stowage drawings and a PDF report. Psychrometric Chart Plot air states on an ASHRAE-style chart and read coil loads, SHR and mixing.
